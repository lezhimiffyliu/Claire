# Integration by Parts

## Pattern Recognition
- Product of two different types of functions
- One factor becomes simpler when differentiated
- LIATE rule: Log, Inverse trig, Algebraic, Trig, Exponential

## Solving Template
1. Choose u and dv using LIATE (u = earlier in LIATE)
2. Compute du = (derivative of u)
3. Compute v = ∫dv
4. Apply formula: ∫udv = uv - ∫vdu
5. Evaluate the remaining integral
6. Add +C for indefinite integrals

## Common Mistakes
- Wrong choice of u and dv
- Forgetting the minus sign in the formula
- Not recognizing when to apply parts twice (tabular method)
